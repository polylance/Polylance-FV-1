Smart Contract Escrow Guide for Web3 Freelancing

An in-depth guide to smart contract milestone escrow for Web3 developers and clients. Learn about automated releases, dispute handling, and non-custodial payouts.

Published: 2026-09-20T00:00:00Z · 8 min read · By PolyLance Core Team
## Smart Contract Escrow Fundamentals In digital freelancing, smart contracts replace custodial middleman accounts with self-executing code. When entering an engagement on PolyLance, milestone terms and payment balances are programmed into `JobEscrow.sol` deployed on Polygon Mainnet. Once funded by the client, funds are locked immutably. Neither party can seize or redirect the principal unilaterally, ensuring absolute cryptographic certainty for both participants. ## Milestone Lifecycle & Release Conditions 1. **Terms Agreement**: The client and freelancer define scope, milestones, deadlines, and delivery formats. 2. **Escrow Funding**: The client deposits the milestone principal plus the {{PLATFORM_FEE_PERCENT}} protocol service fee. 3. **Delivery Submission**: The freelancer completes the milestone deliverable and uploads verifiable proof-of-work with IPFS metadata hashes. 4. **Approval & Payout**: The client inspects the deliverables and approves the release. The escrow smart contract deducts the {{PLATFORM_FEE_PERCENT}} freelancer fee and transfers the net amount instantly to the freelancer's wallet. 5. **Soulbound Certification**: The protocol mints an ERC-5192 Soulbound Reputation Token to the freelancer's wallet, recording verified project completion on-chain. ## Verifying Proof-of-Work On-Chain Every deliverable submission on PolyLance records an immutable cryptographic hash on Polygon. This provides an audit trail that can be verified independently without reliance on private company servers. ## Review Periods & Auto-Release Safeguards Clients have a 7-day review window following deliverable submission. If a client fails to take action during this period without requesting revisions or raising a dispute, the freelancer can call `claimAutoRelease()` to trigger contract settlement directly on Polygon PoS. ## Best Practices for Clients and Talent - Break complex projects into sequential milestones of 1 to 2 weeks. - Include clear acceptance criteria (test coverage benchmarks, supported browsers, target smart contract networks) in the initial agreement. - Maintain all communications and file hashes on-chain or via authenticated channels to preserve dispute evidence integrity.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are milestone funds secured during a project?

Funds are deposited into an on-chain Polygon smart contract that releases tokens only upon client approval or automated expiration of the review window.

Can escrow be cancelled if work has not started?

Yes. Before escrow is funded, projects can be cancelled freely. After funding, cancellation requires mutual consent or dispute resolution.
