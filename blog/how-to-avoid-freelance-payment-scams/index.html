How to Avoid Freelance Payment Scams in Crypto & Web3

Protect your freelance earnings from common crypto scams, fake escrow sites, signature phishing, and off-platform payment fraud with our security guide.

Published: 2026-09-25T00:00:00Z · 8 min read · By PolyLance Core Team
## Top 5 Freelance Crypto Scams 1. **The "Off-Platform Telegram" Trap**: Scammers post jobs on marketplaces, then urge applicants to move to Telegram or Discord, promising higher pay to bypass fees. Once code is delivered, they vanish. 2. **Fake Escrow Smart Contracts**: Malicious actors send links to cloned websites purporting to hold escrow. Always verify the domain is `https://polylance.codes` and check the contract on Polygonscan. 3. **Malicious Code Auditing Repos**: Clients ask freelancers to debug or audit a repository that contains obfuscated malware designed to drain browser wallet extensions. 4. **Testnet Token Substitution**: Fraudulent clients deposit Sepolia or Amoy testnet tokens while claiming they are Mainnet funds. Always verify the Chain ID (Polygon Mainnet is 137). 5. **Approval / Permit Draining**: Signing an unverified `eth_sign` or unlimited token approval that gives an attacker withdrawal rights. ## Golden Rules for Safe Delivery - Never begin work until you verify the escrow contract status is `Funded` on Polygonscan. - Keep all communication and milestone evidence inside PolyLance. - Only sign messages that explicitly display the domain `polylance.codes` and an EIP-4361 SIWE nonce.
