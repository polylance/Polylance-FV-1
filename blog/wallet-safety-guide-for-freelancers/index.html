Wallet Safety Guide for Freelancers: Seed Phrases & Phishing

Essential Web3 security practices for freelancers. Learn how to protect your private keys, avoid malicious signatures, and use hardware wallets safely.

Published: 2026-10-12T00:00:00Z · 8 min read · By PolyLance Core Team
## The Responsibility of Self-Custody In Web3, you are your own bank. If an attacker gains your seed phrase or tricks you into signing a malicious approval, no customer support team can reverse the blockchain transaction. ## Understanding What You Sign - **SIWE (EIP-4361)**: Safe. A gasless signature verifying you control an address. Always check the domain is `polylance.codes`. - **Token Approval (`approve` / `setApprovalForAll`)**: Caution. Grants a contract permission to withdraw tokens from your wallet. Never approve unknown contracts. - **Contract Execution**: Caution. Inspect the destination address and value in MetaMask before confirming.
